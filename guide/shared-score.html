VexTab guide · Triplets in tab and notation

Follow a tied chord

Play open G, rest for a beat, then hold the two-note chord across the barline. The second chord lists the same strings in reverse order. [tie] carries each pitch to its matching string.

<%- require('./scores/tied-chord.vextab').trimEnd() %>

Choose Narrow the score to split the tie across two rows. Select the tied chord and swap its two marked frets in the editor. Each string should still connect to its own fret across the break.

Read the notation part

Choose Engrave notation part, then Find the chord from the part. The printed chord selects the same source as the chord in the screen score.
