Triplets in tab and notation

Play the first three frets evenly on beat one. The fourth fret lands on beat two. :8 selects eighth notes. ^3^ fits the preceding three into the time of two.

options tab-stems=true
tabstave notation=true time=2/4
notes :8 1-2-3/1 ^3^ :q 4/1 |

Hide the standard staff and find the triplet on the tab. Delete ^3^ and count three straight eighths. The fourth fret now starts halfway through beat two.
