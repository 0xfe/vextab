Measures, pickups and reflow

Write a pickup that keeps its length as the music wraps onto a narrower page.

Flow layout counts each voice against the time signature and breaks at barlines. Here the opening G is a one-quarter pickup in 3/4. The next bar has three beats. Delete its last note to see the diagnostic, then restore it. Use fragment=true for a deliberately incomplete excerpt.

stave time=3/4 pickup=1/4
notes :q G/4 | A-B-C/5 | :h D/5 :q B/4 |

Beam groups and accidental spelling

Choose where a group of eighth notes begins and ends, or let the meter decide.

In fixed layout, options beams=explicit lets square brackets group beamable attacks inside a single bar and voice. Move the brackets below to group the middle three eighths. With beams=auto, the meter supplies the grouping. With beams=off, each note has its own flag. The tilde in B@~/4 makes the written flat cautionary.

options beams=explicit stave time=3/4 notes :8 [ C-D-E/4 ] F/4 [ A/4 B@~/4 ] |

Listen to four beats on a nylon-string guitar, then slow them down and try a different instrument.

Colour a stave, an attack or a chord label

Use colour to show a student which attack to listen for.

Independent voices and ties

Let a melody cross a barline while the bass keeps its own pulse.

Start the upper part with voice=top. A separate voice voice=bottom line goes back to the start of the same row. Both voices must fill the same bars. Prefix the arriving pitch or chord with T to tie matching heads across the boundary.

stave time=2/4 voice=top
notes :q E/5 G/5 | T G/5 E/5 |
voice voice=bottom
notes :h C/4 | D/4 |

Bends and palm muting by string

Bend one string of a chord while its neighbor keeps sounding.

The half note below stops strings two and three. !bend 3 +2 raises string three by two semitones. The fractions specify how far through that attack the rise, hold and release end. In the next bar, !pm 6! starts a palm mute on the bass string. !end-pm! includes the attack it follows.

options tab-stems=true
tabstave notation=true time=2/4 tuning=dropd
notes :h (5/2.7/3) !bend 3 +2 rise=1/2 hold=3/4 release=1/1! |
notes :q 0/6 !pm 6! 3/6 !end-pm! |

Pick a pre-bent string and release it

Hear a string that is already bent when the pick lands.

options tempo=80 instrument=24
tabstave notation=true time=2/4
notes :q (0/6.7z8z7/3) 7/3 |

Fret 7 on string three, bend to the pitch at fret 8, then pick. The last z7 brings the pitch back down during that same quarter note. The open bass keeps its pitch. Chord frames and lyric syllables

Give a chord its name, its guitar fingering and a place in a lyric.

Put each mark after its attack. The Am frame lists six strings from low to high. An x skips the sixth string and 0 rings an open string. The barre stops strings four and three at fret two. On the next attack, extend=1 holds “on” through one more note.

stave time=4/4
notes :h (0/5.2/4.2/3.1/2.0/1) !chord "Am"! !frame x,0,2,2,1,0 barre=2:4-3! !lyric "Sail"!
notes :q E/5 !lyric "on" extend=1! C/5 |

Rehearsal marks and repeat endings

Mark where to return and give each pass its own ending.

Place !mark "A"! and !ending 1 bars=1! before the first attack of their bars in the top voice. The ending label spans the written number of bars. The repeat barlines still tell the player where to return. Measure numbers follow the written score.

options measure-numbers=true
stave time=2/4
notes =|: !mark "A"! :q C-D/4 | !ending 1 bars=1! E-D/4 =:|
notes !ending 2 bars=1! :h C/4 =|=
