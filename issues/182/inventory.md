Milestone 1 inventories where people write or copy a score, which program consumes it, and how to tell whether it survived translation. The VexTab baseline for this pass is `e138a8f`. The four [editing tasks and worked scores](editing-tasks.md) exercise decisions from the [language proposal](../../docs/grammarv2.md). Feedback from a guitarist and a pianist is pending.

## What to verify in the migrated scores

| Durations, rests, tuplet ratios, slash heads and beams · [rhythm regressions](../../tests/engraving.ts), [meter lesson](../../guide/scores/meter.vextab) | Compare rational onsets and written dots. Three triplet eighths occupy one quarter, including tab alone. Six eighths have different beam groups in 3/4 and 6/8. Hidden rests still take time. |
| Keys, cautionaries, chord-head ownership and ties · [engraving](../../tests/engraving.ts), [tied chord](../../guide/scores/tied-chord.vextab) | Reordering the heads moves the finger and sign with the intended pitch. A tie across a bar sustains only its target head. |
| Tuning, strings, capo, transpose and chord frames · [transposition](../../tests/transpose.ts), [chord workshop](../../guide/scores/chord-workshop.vextab) | Preserve the physical string, sounding pitch, spelling, slash bass and diagram or report the authored unplayable fret. |
| Slides, pre-bends, hammer-ons, taps, releases, strokes and palm mute · [open slides](../../tests/open-slides.ts), [guitar gestures](../../guide/scores/guitar-gestures.vextab), [turnaround](../../guide/scores/drop-d-turnaround.vextab) | Open slides add one diagonal without a second attack. ## Decisions to carry into milestone 2

- **Fret/string, chord marks and independent clocks.** The worked tasks preserve pitch when changing strings, keep fingerings on reordered heads, and edit one piano voice independently. Ask both musicians where they first looked to make each edit.
- **Explicit incomplete music.** The first historical tutorial example teaches an empty staff. Many later examples are fragments. The prototype requires filled bars. Specify how `fragment` and an empty staff engrave before migrating the tutorial, while keeping full-measure diagnostics in measured scores.
- **Existing drafts.** The guide retrieves saved source by chapter and ordinal. Decide how to version or migrate those drafts when v2 becomes the default. Include one restored v1 draft in the release tests.
- **Written copies and playback repeats.** Test whether authors can predict what `times` prints and which source token to edit. Retain the proposed separation from performed repeats and endings unless the musician review finds a clearer spelling.

Milestone 1 was accepted. The editing tasks remain available for recording musician feedback. Milestone 4 will turn this source inventory into an automated corpus check for the converted material.
